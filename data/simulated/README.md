# Simulated data (Kerala 2018 flood scenario)

Generated by `python scripts/simulate_relief_data.py --seed 42`. Re-running with the same seed gives identical files; every assumption is in `scenario_config.json`.

| File | Rows | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `district_locations.csv` | 14 | Approximate district HQ coordinates |
| `road_edges.csv` | 21 | Phase 1 adjacency edges with road distance (straight-line × 1.35) and travel time at 35 km/h. Placeholder until OSM roads are processed. |
| `relief_centres.csv` | 19 | One centre per district, two for districts over 3M people. Starting stock of food kits, water (L), medicine kits, rescue teams, plus vehicles and shelter capacity. |
| `demand_timeline.csv` | 98 | Daily demand per district for 7 days. Displaced people = population × 100-yr flood exposure × 15% × (1 + vulnerability) × day severity (peaks day 3), ±10% noise. Food 1 kit/4 people/day, water 15 L/person/day, medicine 1 kit/50 people/day, 1 rescue team/20k displaced on high-severity days. |
| `road_events.csv` | 32 | Day-by-day road blocked / slowed / reopened events, more likely on edges touching high-exposure districts. Drives dynamic reallocation. |

Design choices: total stock is about 70% of total demand, and it is placed by population rather than flood risk. So supply is scarce and in the wrong places (Alappuzha and Thrissur get hit hardest), which gives the allocation models a real problem to solve.

These are synthetic numbers for prototyping only, not real relief operations data.
